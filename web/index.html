Disk Reclaim

Find what is filling this Mac, and remove only what you choose.

or get it from GitHub Releases

What it does

Exact bytes, not estimates

Walks the disk and counts the blocks each file actually occupies, largest first — so the sizes you act on are real.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No networking code, no telemetry, no update check. What it reads about your disk is never uploaded or reported anywhere.

A proven way back

Files you pick are moved to the Trash, not erased. Every removal records how it comes back before anything is touched.

You choose everything

Nothing goes without you choosing it. Anything without a safe, recorded way to restore it is left where it is.

First launch

Disk Reclaim isn't yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. This is a one-time step — after that it opens normally.
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Disk Reclaim into your Applications folder.
  2. Try to open it. macOS says it "cannot be checked for malicious software" — that's expected for an app this new.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Disk Reclaim.
  4. Confirm once more. From then on it launches with a normal double-click.
