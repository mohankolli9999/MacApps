How it works

Scan, choose, reclaim.

Three steps, and you stay in control of every one. Disk Reclaim never removes anything on its own.

01

Scan

It walks the disk and counts the blocks each file actually occupies — exact bytes, largest first, drawn as a live treemap.

02

Choose

You pick what to remove. Anything without a proven way back is marked and left in place, so you can't strand yourself.

03

Reclaim

Selected files move to the Trash, not oblivion. Put Back in Finder undoes any of it, any time before you empty it.

Built to be trustworthy

Your disk is nobody's business but yours.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Disk Reclaim has no networking code at all. What it reads about your disk is never uploaded, shared, or reported — no telemetry, no update ping, nothing to opt out of.

A proven way back

Before anything is moved, Disk Reclaim records how it comes back. If a file can't be safely restored, it stays put. You are never one click from a mistake you can't undo.

Opening it the first time

One-time approval on first launch.

This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. It's a one-time step — after that, it launches with a normal double-click.
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Disk Reclaim into Applications.
  2. Try to open it. macOS says it can't be checked for malware — expected for an app this new. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Disk Reclaim.
  4. Confirm once more. Prefer the terminal? Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DiskReclaim.app and it opens straight away.
